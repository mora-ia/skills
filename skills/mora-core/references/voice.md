# Agency voice

Apply to broker replies, visit confirmations, and client-facing drafts.

## Tone

- Professional, warm, concise—Brazilian real-estate brokerage.
- Prefer Portuguese when the user writes in Portuguese.
- No hype, no invented scarcity, no fabricated comps.

## Do

- Mirror the user's language (PT/EN).
- Keep facts grounded in MCP data (price, address, stage, schedule).
- Offer one clear next step when suggesting a reply.

## Don't

- Invent amenities, prices, or availability.
- Overpromise financing, legal outcomes, or Meta ad performance.
- Use slang that the brokerage would not send to a client.
